\cvsection[\faUser]{Professional Summary}

\small
Scientific developer and computational tool developer with a research background in mathematical modelling, simulation, optimisation, and reproducible scientific computing across biological domains, from hepatic fatty-acid metabolism and immune-cell dynamics to plant photosynthetic adaptation across the C3 to C4 continuum. Skilled in parameter estimation, optimisation, uncertainty analysis, and scientific computing to translate complex biological questions into interpretable frameworks for hypothesis testing, experimental design, and model-guided decision-making. \role{Postdoctoral Researcher}{Nov 2022 -- Oct 2025}{CEPLAS / Institute of Computational Cell Biology, HHU D\"usseldorf}{\begin{itemize}
\item Built a first-principles thermo-hydraulic-biochemical model of photosynthesis across the C3 to C4 continuum in \textit{Flaveria}, integrating biochemical kinetics, heat transfer, hydraulics, and environmental forcing in one workflow.
\item Combined Sobol sensitivity analysis, large simulation ensembles, and CMA-ES-based optimisation to reveal environment-specific adaptive trait combinations and prioritise candidate traits for crop engineering and model-guided experimental design.
\item Used multiscale modeling to connect mesophyll and bundle-sheath physiology, transport constraints, and environmental response in plant adaptation.
\item Presented this work at the CEPLAS Early Career Researcher Symposium, D\"usseldorf, 2025.
\item Supervised 2~MSc and 2~BSc projects in computational plant biology.
\end{itemize}}

\role{Marie Curie PhD Fellow}{Aug 2019 -- Aug 2023}{Institute of Quantitative and Theoretical Biology, HHU D\"usseldorf}{\begin{itemize}
\item Built mechanistic liver-metabolism models linking fatty-acid synthesis, oxidation, and degradation into interpretable dynamical systems for disease-relevant metabolic behavior.
\item Established analytical conditions for bi-stability in fatty-acid metabolism and fitted semi-mechanistic models to time-course data, clarifying metabolic state switching and when reduced kinetics capture system behavior.
\item Used parameter estimation, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty quantification to strengthen robustness and interpretability in nonlinear biological models.
\item Developed the enzyme-kinetic formalism for differentiable constraint-based metabolic models in a cross-institutional collaboration; published in \textit{Metabolic Engineering} (2022).
\item Compiled and formalised kinetic information for enzymes in animal fatty-acid synthesis; published as first author in \textit{Bioscience Reports} (2023).
\end{itemize}}

\role{Research Fellow}{Nov 2017 -- Mar 2019}{AIMS Cameroon \& AIMS Rwanda}{\begin{itemize}
\item Developed stochastic and deterministic models of T-cell proliferation dynamics to extract interpretable parameters from CFSE-type experiments.
\item Formulated a stochastic master-equation framework, derived analytical expressions for cell-count dynamics, and built a maximum-likelihood estimation workflow for model fitting.
\item Worked across competing model classes, including ODE, delay, branching, and stochastic-process formulations, to assess biological interpretability and fitting behavior.
\end{itemize}}
