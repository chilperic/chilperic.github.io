\cvsection[\faUser]{Profile}
\small

Computational systems biologist, applied mathematician, and scientific developer building mechanistic models and computational tools for biological systems, from hepatic metabolism and immune-cell dynamics to photosynthetic adaptation across the C3 to C4 continuum. My work combines dynamical systems theory, stochastic modeling, and biological interpretation, with a focus on what mathematics can explain about living systems, not just fit. I also develop scientific and educational web tools that make simulation, analysis, and technical training more accessible.

\cvsection[\faBriefcase]{Research Experience}
\role{Independent Research, Scientific Software, and Further Training}{Nov 2025 -- Present}{D\"usseldorf, Germany}{\begin{itemize}
\item Preparing two manuscripts from postdoctoral research on the C3 to C4 continuum: a first-principles thermo-hydraulic framework for photosynthetic adaptation and a structure-function framework for leaf photosynthesis.
\item Developed browser-based tools for scientific modeling and language learning, including Foko Lab and Deutsch-WiPA 2026.
\item Pursuing structured upskilling in AI engineering and professional German to strengthen research-software development and scientific work in German-speaking environments.
\end{itemize}}

\role{Postdoctoral Researcher}{Nov 2022 -- Oct 2025}{CEPLAS / Institute of Computational Cell Biology, HHU D\"usseldorf}{\begin{itemize}
\item Developed a first-principles thermo-hydraulic-biochemical modeling framework for photosynthetic adaptation across the C3 to C4 continuum in \textit{Flaveria}, linking mesophyll and bundle-sheath physiology to soil and environment.
\item Coupled C3 to C4 FvCB biochemistry, heat balance, hydraulic regulation, and environmental forcing to explain how anatomy, transport, and physiology jointly shape photosynthetic performance.
\item Applied Sobol sensitivity analysis and CMA-ES-based evolutionary simulations across contrasting climatic regimes to identify influential traits, quantify trade-offs, and show that different climates favor different adaptive routes rather than a single universal optimum.
\item Presented this work at the CEPLAS Early Career Researcher Symposium, D\"usseldorf, 2025.
\item Supervised 2~MSc and 2~BSc projects in computational plant biology.
\end{itemize}}

\role{Marie Curie PhD Fellow}{Aug 2019 -- Aug 2023}{Institute of Quantitative and Theoretical Biology, HHU D\"usseldorf}{\begin{itemize}
\item Developed dynamic and stochastic models of hepatic fatty-acid metabolism to clarify regulatory mechanisms underlying metabolite dynamics and disease-relevant metabolic behavior.
\item Built a coarse-grained model of fatty-acid metabolism and derived conditions for bi-stability, providing a mechanistic explanation for metabolic switching between fed and fasted states.
\item Developed a semi-mechanistic model of fatty-acid de novo synthesis, fitted it to time-course data, and showed when production rates can be approximated by Michaelis--Menten laws, improving kinetic interpretability.
\item Performed parameter estimation, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty analysis for nonlinear biological systems to strengthen mechanistic interpretation of hepatic fatty-acid metabolism.
\item Developed the enzyme-kinetic component of a differentiable constraint-based metabolic modeling framework, formalizing how kinetic constraints integrate into systems-level metabolic analysis; published in \textit{Metabolic Engineering} (2022).
\end{itemize}}

\role{Research Fellow}{Nov 2017 -- Mar 2019}{AIMS Cameroon \& AIMS Rwanda}{\begin{itemize}
\item Modeled T-cell proliferation dynamics using stochastic and deterministic frameworks, with emphasis on branching processes, individual-based simulation, and moment-based analysis.
\item Developed a stochastic master-equation framework for T-cell proliferation, derived mean and variance expressions for cell counts, and set up a maximum-likelihood fitting framework for CFSE-type data.
\end{itemize}}
