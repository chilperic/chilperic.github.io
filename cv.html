Profile

Computational systems biologist, applied mathematician, and scientific developer building mechanistic models and computational tools for biological systems, from hepatic metabolism and immune-cell dynamics to photosynthetic adaptation across the C3 to C4 continuum. My work combines dynamical systems theory, stochastic modelling, and biological interpretation, with a focus on what mathematics can explain about living systems, not just fit.

The common thread is building interpretable computational systems that people can run, test, falsify, and extend.
